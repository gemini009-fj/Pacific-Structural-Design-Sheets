Questions

Which Excel version do I need?

Microsoft Excel 2016 or later, or Microsoft 365, on Windows or Mac. The sheets use no macros, so there are no security warnings. Excel on phones opens them, but charts and drawings work best on a computer.

Can I see the formulas?

Yes. Every result is a visible Excel formula with its clause reference. Cells are locked against accidental edits, not hidden.

What units are used?

SI units throughout: kN, kPa, m, mm and MPa, with the unit shown beside every value.

Do I get updates?

Yes. Fixes and improvements to a sheet you bought are emailed to you free of charge.

What if a file has a problem?

If a file doesn't open or you find an error, contact us. We will fix it and send you a corrected version, or refund you.

How do I pay?

Email your order and we'll reply with payment details. In Fiji, pay in FJD by M-PAiSA. From overseas, send by Wise (to a Fiji account or M-PAiSA) or by international bank transfer. Card checkout is coming soon.
